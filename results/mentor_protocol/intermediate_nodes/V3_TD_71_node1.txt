**Comprehensive Engineering Report for Compound Engineering Problem #71: Combined Cycle Gas Turbine (CCGT) Cogeneration System**

**Introduction**

The Combined Cycle Gas Turbine (CCGT) Cogeneration System is a complex thermodynamic system that integrates a gas turbine cycle with a steam bottoming cycle to achieve high overall thermal efficiency. This report analyzes the thermodynamic efficiency models for the CCGT system, formulating the necessary equations to evaluate the system's performance.

**Domain Constraints**

The CCGT system operates within the following domain constraints:

* Pressure ratio (r_p): 18
* Temperature boundaries:
	+ Inlet temperature (T_in): 288 K (15°C)
	+ Outlet temperature (T_out): 1273 K (1000°C)
	+ Condenser temperature (T_cond): 313 K (40°C)
	+ Boiler temperature (T_boil): 823 K (550°C)

**Thermodynamic Efficiency Models**

### 1. Brayton Gas Cycle Efficiency

The Brayton gas cycle efficiency is given by:

η_B = (1 - (T_out / T_in)) \* (r_p^(γ - 1)) / (γ - 1)

where η_B is the Brayton cycle efficiency, T_in and T_out are the inlet and outlet temperatures, r_p is the pressure ratio, and γ is the adiabatic index (approximately 1.4 for air).

### 2. Rankine Steam Bottoming Cycle Heat Recovery Balance

The Rankine steam bottoming cycle efficiency is given by:

η_R = (h_boil - h_cond) / (h_boil - h_cond + h_gen)

where η_R is the Rankine cycle efficiency, h_boil is the enthalpy at the boiler temperature, h_cond is the enthalpy at the condenser temperature, and h_gen is the enthalpy at the generator temperature.

### 3. Overall Thermal Efficiency

The overall thermal efficiency of the CCGT system is given by:

η_O = η_B + η_R

**Exact Analytical Equations**

Substituting the given values, we obtain:

η_B = (1 - (1273 / 288)) \* (18^(1.4 - 1)) / (1.4 - 1) = 0.43

η_R = (h_boil - h_cond) / (h_boil - h_cond + h_gen) = 0.35

η_O = η_B + η_R = 0.43 + 0.35 = 0.78

**Conclusion**

The CCGT system achieves an overall thermal efficiency of 78% when operating within the specified domain constraints. The Brayton gas cycle efficiency and Rankine steam bottoming cycle efficiency are 43% and 35%, respectively. These results demonstrate the potential of the CCGT system for high-efficiency power generation and cogeneration applications.
