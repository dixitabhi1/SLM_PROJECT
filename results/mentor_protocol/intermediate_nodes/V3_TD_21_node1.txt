**State Space**

1. **Follower**: A node that is not the leader and is not a candidate.
2. **Candidate**: A node that is not the leader but is in the process of becoming the leader.
3. **Leader**: The node that is currently responsible for making decisions.

Term monotonicity ensures that the term (a unique identifier) of a node increases as it transitions from a follower to a candidate and eventually to a leader. This property guarantees that each node's term is strictly greater than its previous term.

**Election Safety Invariant**

The election safety invariant states that at most one leader exists per term. This means that if a node becomes the leader, it will not be replaced by another node until the term changes.

**Quorum Intersection Property**

The quorum intersection property ensures that a node becomes a leader only if it has received a quorum of votes from other nodes. This property guarantees that the leader is elected by a majority of the nodes.
