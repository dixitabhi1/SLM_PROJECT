**Engineering Problem #1: 2D Transient Thermal Conduction and Convection in an Aerospace Heat Sink**

**Quantitative Formulation:**

The 2D transient thermal conduction and convection system can be described by the following heat equation:

∂T/∂t = α (∂²T/∂x² + ∂²T/∂y²) + h_c (T - T_∞)

where:

* T(x,y,t) is the temperature at position (x,y) and time t
* α is the thermal diffusivity of the material
* h_c is the convective heat transfer coefficient
* T_∞ is the ambient temperature
* ∂²T/∂x² and ∂²T/∂y² are the second derivatives of temperature with respect to x and y, respectively

**Boundary Conditions:**

* Dirichlet boundary condition at the heat source base: T(x,y,t) = T_base
* Robin convective boundary condition at the cooling fins: -k (∂T/∂n) = h_c (T - T_∞)

where:

* k is the thermal conductivity of the material
* n is the unit normal vector to the boundary

**Dimensionless Numbers:**

* Biot number (Bi): Bi = h_c L / k, where L is the characteristic length
* Fourier number (Fo): Fo = α t / L², where t is the time

**Explicit Finite-Difference Numerical Stability Condition:**

The explicit finite-difference method is conditionally stable, and the stability condition is given by:

Fo ≤ 0.25

This means that the time step Δt must be smaller than or equal to 0.25 times the characteristic time scale α L² / k.

N_x = 100
N_y = 100

# Grid spacing
dx = L / (N_x - 1)
