1. Executive Summary & Experimental Objectives

Experiment 2 (E2) investigates the empirical impact of Query-Dependent Specialist Fine-Tuning under the Mentor Experiment Protocol. In contrast to Experiment 1 (E1) where all SLM pool models were un-adapted, E2 selectively routes subtasks based on query domain requirements: technical coding/systems subtasks are routed to phi3.5-ft-coding:latest (3.82B parameters, local RTX 3050 GPU), while general and analytical formulation tasks are executed by the base general specialist (meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct). All 4 Baselines remain strictly un-adapted and non-fine-tuned, matched directly against cached E1 baseline responses for 100% paired-trial fidelity.
